RadNote Source Attribution Notes

RadNote is designed around source-linked study exports. The goal is to make review materials easier to revisit while keeping the original Radiopaedia context close to the learner.

Attribution habits

  • Keep the original source URL visible in exported Anki cards, PDF study sheets, and case conference slide outlines.
  • Use RadNote exports as personal study aids, not as a replacement for reading the source page.
  • Reopen the original case or article when details need clinical, educational, or presentation review.
  • Respect source licensing, attribution requirements, and institutional teaching policies.

RadNote is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Radiopaedia. It supports reviewed study workflows for users who want source links preserved in their own notes.
