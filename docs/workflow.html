RadNote Workflow Notes

RadNote supports a focused workflow for radiology learners who want to turn reviewed source material into repeatable study assets.

Typical Steps

  1. Open a Radiopaedia article or case that is appropriate for personal study.
  2. Review the content and decide what should become a study card, PDF sheet, or presentation outline.
  3. Use RadNote to prepare exports while keeping source links and attribution in the output.
  4. Import the Anki deck, review the PDF study sheet, or adapt the case conference slide outline.

Public Links

The extension is available on the Chrome Web Store. The public support repository is at GitHub.
