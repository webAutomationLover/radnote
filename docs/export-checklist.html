RadNote Export Checklist

This checklist is for learners using RadNote to prepare structured review material from selected Radiopaedia articles and cases. It focuses on clean exports, attribution, and later review.

Before exporting

  • Confirm the source page is the correct article or case for the study session.
  • Review the selected material before creating flashcards or slides.
  • Keep the original source link and attribution visible in the output.

After exporting

  • Open the generated Anki deck and check that cards remain concise.
  • Review the PDF study sheet for headings, source links, and context.
  • Check case conference slides before presenting them in a teaching setting.
  • Return to the original source when details need verification.

RadNote supports personal study workflows. It does not replace professional judgment, source review, or institutional content policies.
